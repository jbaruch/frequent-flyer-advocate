# Escalation Document Requirements

Every escalation guide, escalation plan, or next-steps document MUST include:

- **Compensation logging.** Tell the user to come back when they hear from the airline so compensation can be logged to the credits inventory and a follow-up or DOT complaint drafted if needed.
- **Submission destination.** Give the verified form link and navigation choices, email recipient address, or mailing address that matches the draft.
- **Executive routing.** If general customer service did not resolve the complaint, give a verified executive customer relations destination when available. Otherwise give the verified follow-up route and say which contact could not be confirmed.
